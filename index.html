Papirfly solved its first two strategic problems — unifying the platform, and sharpening sales & marketing. The third, and most consequential, is now: making customers wildly successful, fast, at scale. Three core initiatives, one operating system, twelve months.

01

The shift that reframes the role

Two funnels, not one. Most of the value lives in the second.

The first funnel ends at contract signed. The second is where compounding value lives.

Papirfly's previous two iterations of "#1 problem" both lived in the first funnel — getting attention, building positioning, closing deals. That work is largely solved.

The current problem set — slow time-to-value, weak feature adoption, fragmented CS processes — all live in the second funnel: Activate → Adopt → Advocate → Expand. This is where Net Revenue Retention is won or lost. In SaaS, NRR is now a bigger valuation lever than new logo growth.

Product Marketing's playbook — narrative, personas, enablement, cross-functional orchestration — translates one-for-one into the second funnel. The role doesn't change. The audience does: from prospects to customers, CSMs, and CS leaders.
Two funnels · one customer

Three core initiatives

Each maps to a stage of the second funnel. Together they form a closed loop.

01
The Activation EngineFirst 90 days · Time-to-value
For new customers
The problem we're solving

New customers spend too long getting to first measurable value. Five flagship products from M&A history means five different onboarding shapes — and no single playbook.

Done well, this is the highest-leverage initiative: it reduces churn risk in the first 12 months, strengthens expansion conversations later, and signals to the CS team that the new operating model works.

What we build
  • A defined "first value milestone" per product / use case
  • One onboarding playbook per top vertical (initially 3)
  • In-product activation flows — checklists, tooltips, milestone celebrations
  • Self-serve content library (academy, video, templates)
  • Tiered onboarding: white-glove for top ARR, programmatic for the rest

03

How we scale: tiered, not flat

The same initiatives delivered three ways — matched to customer value and complexity.

One playbook, three delivery modes.

The trap most CS organizations fall into is treating every customer the same way — either over-investing in long-tail accounts, or under-serving strategic ones. The fix is tiered delivery, where each initiative shows up differently for each tier.

The pyramid below shows the headcount reality: most customer effort goes to the long tail through 1:many digital programs, freeing CSMs to be actually high-touch with the strategic accounts that drive the majority of ARR.

This is the model that makes "scalable" real. Without it, "scaling customer success" is just hiring more CSMs.

TIER 1 · 1:1
Dedicated CSM, exec sponsor, custom QBRs

Top ARR, strategic logos, reference candidates. Bespoke onboarding. Named contact in every function.

TIER 2 · 1:FEW
Pooled CSM, group office hours, vertical cohorts

Mid-market growth segment. Programmatic onboarding by industry. Quarterly group health checks.

TIER 3 · 1:MANY
Digital-led, in-app guidance, academy & community

Long tail. Tech-touch CS. Triggered email journeys. Self-serve KB and video library do the work.
